Popular Companies

Nike

5/10

Pros

  • "Move to Zero" campaign for zero carbon and waste
  • Uses recycled materials and runs "Nike Grind" program
  • Plans to eliminate dangerous chemicals by 2025

Cons

  • Still uses environmentally harmful materials like leather and wool
  • Many supply chain workers don't receive living wages
  • Limited transparency about supply chain practices

Researched by: Joshua

Adidas

6.5/10

Pros

  • "End Plastic Waste" initiative helps reduce pollution
  • Uses recycled materials, including ocean plastics
  • Transparent about suppliers and factory oversight

Cons

  • Still uses leather and other harmful materials
  • Not all supply chain workers earn living wages
  • Could improve faster on labor rights issues

Researched by: Ruben

Puma

7/10

Pros

  • Science-based targets to reduce greenhouse gases
  • Transforms textile waste into new materials
  • Contributing to a more circular economy

Cons

  • Poor performance on sustainable materials usage
  • Recycled materials limited to small portion of products
  • Only 20% recycled materials in some shoe uppers

Researched by: Matthew

Sustainable Alternatives

Footwear Alternative

Allbirds

Allbirds is one of the most eco-friendly shoe brands, using natural materials like merino wool, eucalyptus, and sugarcane instead of plastic. Each pair is labeled with its carbon footprint, and the company offsets all emissions to stay carbon neutral. Their simple, comfy design and low environmental impact make them a smart alternative to big sneaker brands.

Carbon Neutral

Clothing Alternative

Pangaia

Pangaia is a top eco-friendly clothing brand that uses innovative materials like seaweed fiber and recycled cotton to reduce waste and water use. They treat clothes with peppermint oil to cut down on washing and aim for carbon neutrality by offsetting emissions. Their science-based approach makes them one of the most sustainable options in fashion.
